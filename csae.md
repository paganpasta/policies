Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

Effective Date: 06/10/2026

Last Updated: 06/10/2026

Application Name: Grafyty ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our")
1. Zero-Tolerance Statement

Grafyty maintains an absolute, unyielding zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to keeping children safe and ensuring our platform is never used to harm, exploit, or endanger minors.

Any user who uploads, creates, solicits, stores, transmits, promotes, or facilitates CSAE content or behavior through Grafyty will face immediate and permanent account termination, a permanent ban from accessing our services, and mandatory reporting to law enforcement authorities and international child protection organizations.
2. Scope & Applicability

This policy applies to all users, registered accounts, content creators, and visitors accessing or interacting with Grafyty. It covers all communications and media types within or facilitated by the Application, including:

    User profiles, avatars, usernames, bios, and header images.

    Text messages, comments, chat interactions, and direct messages.

    Images, videos, live streams, audio recordings, and file attachments.

    External hyperlinks, cloud storage links, or references to third-party platforms.

    AI-generated, synthetic, or digitally manipulated media depicting or simulating minors.

3. Definition of CSAE & CSAM

In accordance with global child protection laws and Google Play Developer Policies, CSAE and CSAM include, but are not limited to:

    Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual depiction (photograph, video, digital art, or computer-generated image) of a minor (any individual under the age of 18, or the applicable legal age of majority) engaged in explicit sexual conduct, real or simulated.

    Child Sexual Exploitation: Any behavior, communication, or transaction that exploits, sexualizes, coerces, threatens, or manipulates a minor for sexual gratification, financial gain, or leverage.

    Child Grooming: Predatory communication, trust-building, or manipulation directed toward a minor with the intent to establish a sexual relationship, arrange a physical meeting, or elicit sexually explicit content.

    Sexualization of Minors: Content or commentary that inappropriately sexualizes children or depicts minors in sexually suggestive poses, attire, or contexts.

    Solicitation & Trafficking: Offering, requesting, buying, selling, trading, or distributing sexual content involving minors, or facilitating the human trafficking of children.

4. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users are strictly prohibited from performing any of the following actions on or through Grafyty:

    Uploading, publishing, distributing, storing, or sharing any CSAM or CSAE content.

    Asking, encouraging, or coercing minors to share nude, semi-nude, or sexually explicit photos, videos, or audio.

    Sharing links, dark web destinations, or external file-sharing directories containing or advertising CSAE material.

    Initiating contact or engaging in conversation with minors for predatory, abusive, or grooming purposes.

    Creating, generating, or sharing synthetic or AI-rendered content depicting minors in sexualized or explicit situations.

5. Detection, Moderation, and Enforcement

To enforce this policy rigorously, Grafyty employs proactive and reactive safety measures:

    Automated Screening & Filtering: We utilize industry-standard automated tools, perceptual hashing technologies (such as PhotoDNA or equivalent CSAM detection APIs), and text-filtering algorithms to detect, block, and prevent known CSAE content from being published.

    Human Review: Our dedicated moderation team reviews flagged accounts, suspicious activity reports, and high-risk content queues.

    Immediate Enforcement Actions: Upon identifying or receiving a credible report of CSAE:

        The offending content is permanently erased from public and private storage.

        The offending user account is permanently terminated.

        Device fingerprints, IP addresses, and user identifiers associated with the violator are permanently blocked from re-registering.

6. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC

Grafyty complies with mandatory reporting requirements under applicable domestic and international laws:

    NCMEC CyberTipline: Pursuant to United States federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) and global safety standards, we report all instances of CSAM/CSAE identified on our services to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline.

    Global Law Enforcement Cooperation: Where applicable, reports are forwarded to international child protection networks (e.g., INHOPE member hotlines) and relevant local or national cybercrime law enforcement agencies.

    Data Preservation: We preserve relevant user account records, log files, IP addresses, communications, and media evidence associated with reported CSAE violations to assist law enforcement agencies in active investigations and legal prosecutions.
